How Players Make Decisions

Match Logic & Decision-Making Process

The Decision-Making Hierarchy

Every match follows a constant cycle of decisions:

1. SYNC STATE Update possession and match status 2. UPDATE TEAM SHAPE (BOTH TEAMS) Every player gets a target position Attackers support carrier, defenders mark opponents 3. DECIDE ACTION (Ball Carrier Only) Pick: Chance attempt? Shot? Through ball? Pass? Dribble? Each option has a success probability 4. RESOLVE ACTION Pass: Does receiver catch it or defender intercept? Shot: Does goalkeeper save or does it go in? Dribble: Does carrier beat defender one-on-one? Chance: Does risky opportunity create a goal-scoring chance? 5. ANIMATE Show the action playing out on screen 6. NEXT DECISION TICK (Every 0.2-0.3 seconds) Repeat cycle

Player Roles

GOALKEEPER (GK) Decides: Should I attempt to save this shot?
CENTER BACKS (CB) Decides: Should I tackle this dribbler? Clear the ball?
FULLBACKS (FB) — Left/Right Decides: Help defend wing? Push forward to cross? Cut inside?
DEFENSIVE MIDFIELDER (DM) Decides: Stay deep and screen? Press forward? Recycle possession? Support attack?
CENTER MIDFIELDER (CM) Decides: Support attack or defense? Make a pass? Drive forward? Box run?
ATTACKING MIDFIELDER (AM) Decides: Create a chance? Play a through ball? Attempt a risky pass? Score?
WINGER (W) — Left/Right Decides: Cross from wing? Cut inside? Take defender on? Shoot?
STRIKER (ST) Decides: Shoot now? Wait for better chance? Make a pass?

What is "Probing"?

Probing means attempting a risky, creative chance — not a regular pass or shot, but a "chance attempt" that might create something special.

When probing: Player tries something ambitious (a risky pass, a flick over defender, a sudden shot from distance). Success rate is lower, but if it works, creates a high-quality chance.

More likely when: Attacking midfielder/creator has the ball in final third with confidence.

How Each Decision is Made

The actual logic behind actions

Decision 1: Should I Attempt a Risky Chance (Probe)?

Condition Result
Only in FINAL THIRD (attacking nearest goal) Check this decision
Am I an attacker archetype? (high xa90, key_passes90) More likely to probe
Did my last few probes succeed? More likely to probe again
How many actions have I taken in this possession? Pressure builds to be creative

If probing succeeds: Chance is created for striker in box.

If probing fails: Possession lost, turnover to defense.

Decision 2: Should I Shoot?

Condition Result
Am I in the penalty box (y > 80)? Can shoot
Do I have space to shoot? How close is defender? Better space = higher shot probability
My shooting ability (goals90, composure) Better shooters take more shots
Am I a goal-scorer? (high goals90) Strikers, goal-scoring wingers/midfielders shoot more

If shot succeeds: Goalkeeper tries to save. If save fails, GOAL.

If shot fails: Miss or saved, possession to defense.

Decision 3: Should I Play a Through Ball?

Condition Result
Is a teammate making a run (arriving into space)? Through ball option available
Are defenders between me and the running teammate? Higher risk, higher reward
My passing accuracy and vision (xa90, key_passes90) Better creators execute through balls more
Am I an attacker archetype? Elite creators attempt more through balls

If through ball succeeds: Teammate receives in good position, can shoot.

If through ball fails: Intercepted by defender, turnover.

Decision 4: Should I Pass?

Default action. Find the best receiver:

  • Who is open? (away from defenders)
  • Who has good positioning? (ready to receive)
  • How far is the pass? (shorter is safer)
  • Is a defender blocking the passing lane?

If pass succeeds: Receiver gets the ball, cycle continues.

If pass intercepted: Defender gets ball, possession changes.

Decision 5: Should I Dribble?

When no good passing option exists, try to beat the nearest defender one-on-one:

Factor Helps You Win Helps Defender Win
Dribbling skill High dribbles90, dribble_pct N/A
Confidence (recent wins) Just beat someone Been beaten recently
Defender's tackling Low tackles90 High tackles90
Defender's duel-winning Low duels_won_pct High duels_won_pct
Defender's positioning Far away Close and reading play

If dribble succeeds: You beat defender, advance the ball. Defender exposed for 2 seconds.

If dribble fails: Defender wins tackle, possession lost. May be a foul (depends on situation).

Midfielder & Attacker Archetypes

How different player types behave

MIDFIELDER ARCHETYPES (DM/CM)

These midfielders focus on controlling the game, defending, and supporting play. Not primarily about scoring.

Anchor DM
Positioning specialist who reads the game well
Engages only when timing is perfect
Best for: Screening central zone, protecting defense
Ball-Winner DM
Aggressive, attacks the ball immediately, wins possession back quickly
Can be exposed if beaten
Best for: Pressing high, disrupting play
Box-to-Box CM
High mobility, balanced defense
Covers ground, supports both attack and defense
Flexible positional coverage
Best for: Transition play, adapting to match flow
Playmaking CM
Prefers positioning over physical engagement
Breaks up play through intelligence
Best for: Possession-based defenses, tempo control

ATTACKER ARCHETYPES (AM/W/ST)

These players focus on creating chances and scoring goals. Primary job is chance creation and finishing.

Elite Chance Creator
Probes frequently, plays through balls often, takes risks
Best for: Breaking down organized defenses
Volume Creator
Safe, recycling passes, builds possession steadily
Best for: Controlling tempo, wearing down opponents
Selective Elite Creator
Patient, waits for perfect moment, then commits fully
Best for: Creating high-quality chances, less volume
Progressive Creator
Carries ball forward, drives attack from deep, ball-carrier focused
Best for: Transitioning from defense to attack

HYBRID ARCHETYPES

Some players have unconventional stat combinations, creating unique tactical profiles:

Goal-Scoring Attacker (Winger/AM)
Creates chances AND scores frequently, not just one or the other
Shoots more than typical wingers/AMs — a dual threat
Best for: Teams needing attacking overload, one-player threats
Box-Crashing Midfielder (CM)
Scores like a striker but defends like a midfielder
Makes forward runs into box AND wins physical contests
Best for: Adding attacking threat from midfield without losing defense

Defender Archetypes & Squad Balance

Building a complete team

DEFENDER ARCHETYPES (CB/FB)

Center Backs

Dominant Defender
Aggressive, commits to challenges immediately
Physical presence in defense, wins aerial duels
Best for: Man-marking systems, aggressive pressing
Positioning Specialist
Stands in right place, cuts out passes before they arrive
Lower tackle rate but very effective reading
Best for: Zonal defense, protecting goal, deep defending

Fullbacks (Left/Right Back)

Attacking Fullback
Joins attack constantly, overlaps with winger
Can cut inside (underlap) for combinations
Pushes high when team attacks
Still covers defensively but less conservative
Best for: Attacking teams, dynamic width play
Defensive Fullback
Stays deep to protect center backs
Tucks into midfield when needed
Sits back even when team attacks
Provides width but doesn't leave flank unguarded
Rare overlap attempts, prefers underlapping
Best for: Defensive teams, protecting goal

What Makes Decisions Work

Decision Type Depends On Success Rate
Pass Passer accuracy, receiver positioning, pressure ~70-85% base
Through Ball Passer quality, teammate run timing, defender density ~50-75%
Probe/Chance Player confidence, finisher type, team momentum ~30-50%
Shot Shooter finishing ability, space, goalkeeper quality ~20-45%
Dribble Dribbler vs defender skill comparison, positioning ~45-55%

Squad Composition Guidelines

  • Attacker + Finisher Match: Elite creator needs clinical finisher to convert chances.
  • Hybrid Players Add Value: Goal-scoring winger doesn't need a striker. Box-crashing midfielder increases midfield threat.
  • Defense Stability: Anchor DM + positioning CBs = solid base. Ball-winner DM needs good supporting defenders.
  • Tempo Control: Playmaking CM + volume creator = possession. Progressive creator + aggressive defense = transitions.
  • Width: Fullbacks should complement wingers (crossing support, covering).

Key Principles

  • Every decision depends on the player's actual stats, not just archetype.
  • Archetypes modify behavior, not replace it.
  • A clinical finisher without good supply will underperform.
  • An anchor DM without defensive protection can be exploited.
  • Hybrid players (goal-scoring wingers, box-crashing midfielders) add unpredictability and flexibility.
  • Team balance matters more than individual stars.
